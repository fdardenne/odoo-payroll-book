Before you start

Who you will meet, and how the missions work

After this chapter you can
  • Recognise the five people of MyVidangeCompany and what each one teaches
  • Open a master database on runbot and play a mission

You work on Odoo payroll and may never have run a payroll yourself. This book goes from theory (what a payslip is) to localization (what changes between countries), then Odoo (hands-on, then the engine), and ends with building and reviewing a localization.

One company runs through the book: MyVidangeCompany, in Belgium, Odoo's most complete payroll localization. You need no Belgian law: terms are explained where they appear, and the In Belgium boxes can be skipped.

Meet MyVidangeCompany

MyVidangeCompany is a software company in Namur. It pays monthly and its office employees work a 38-hour week. Five of them carry the book:

Person Role, monthly gross Teaches Net, Oct 2026: the law Net, Oct 2026: Odoo
Mélanie Developer, 4,000.00 €, single, no children the standard case 2,614.26 2,614.27
Jurgen Support agent, 2,300.00 € a low wage: contribution relief that fades out as pay rises 2,143.05 2,143.06
Marie Engineering manager, 7,500.00 €, married, two children, company car, meal vouchers a high earner, benefits in kind, family situation in the tax 4,663.91 4,684.68
Yannick Designer, 3,600.00 €, joins on 12 March 2026 proration, the hiring declaration, a first year without holiday rights 2,444.37 2,444.37
Emma Ops lead, 5,000.00 €, hired 1 Oct 2019, dismissed on 31 October 2026 the leaver: final pay, notice indemnity, exit holiday pay 3,051.72 3,159.73

Every payslip was computed twice.1,2 An independent calculator written from the law gives the numbers of Part I; the payslips Odoo master produced give those of Parts II to V. Where they differ, the chapter says why (Chapter 10).

Key idea

Two independent computations of the same payslip are the cheapest test a localization can have. Where they agree, the payslip is right. Where they differ, someone learns something about the law or about the code.

The missions: on the master runbot

Part III is played, not read. Each chapter ends with missions: a short story, a goal, a number to check and a hint. They follow MyVidangeCompany through 2026 on one database you build from an empty Odoo, with only a browser and Odoo's runbot.

Take the base database of an enterprise build: it holds only the base apps, like a new customer. Runbot's "today" is the real date, so mission dates are typed explicitly.

Mission 0.1 · Open a master build ★

Situation. You have just joined MyVidangeCompany's payroll team. There is no payroll database yet.

Your goal. Open a fresh master database on runbot and log in as the administrator.

Check. You are logged in as Mitchell Admin; the only company is YourCompany, and the Payroll app is not installed yet.

Hint

Take the base database of an enterprise build, not the one with all modules.
