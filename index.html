Welcome

This is the web edition of Payroll, from Theory to Odoo Localization, a study book for product owners and developers who know Odoo but not payroll. It starts from the ideas every country's payroll shares, follows one Belgian company through Odoo's salary engine, and ends with a template for designing, building and reviewing a localization for any country.

Read it chapter by chapter from the contents below, or search it from the sidebar. Two figures are interactive on this site: the gross-to-net bars of Chapter 2 and the payslip stepper of Chapter 19.

Part II · Localization theory

What changes from one country to the next, how to find out what the law says, and how to keep up when it moves. The comparison points are Odoo's own localizations, from the United States, India and Mexico to the Gulf, Australia, Switzerland, Hong Kong and Kenya, read from the code. Belgium is one worked example among them.
